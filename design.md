# Mockup Outreach CRM Design System

## Direction

Mockup Outreach CRM should feel like a premium internal operating system: calm, precise, spacious, and built for confident review work. The product surface is not a marketing site. It is an operational command center for importing researched prospects, inspecting public mockups and social audits, approving email drafts, and controlling manual sends.

## Principles

- Clarity first: every page should answer what needs attention, what is blocked, and what is safe to do next.
- Restraint over flash: off-white surfaces, graphite text, fine borders, and small color signals carry the experience.
- Trust before action: approval and send contexts must expose sender, mode, destination, artifact, warnings, and caps before risky controls.
- Hierarchy through spacing: use strong page headers, section cards, compact status pills, and quieter supporting metadata.
- Responsive operations: tables can scroll on desktop, but mobile should have readable stacked rows, no clipped headings, and thumb-sized controls.

## Visual Tokens

- Background: off-white paper with white content surfaces.
- Typography: system sans, clear size differences, tabular numerals for metrics.
- Radius: 8-14px for operational controls and cards.
- Depth: hairline borders and subtle shadows only.
- Status: blue for active/system, green for ready/configured, amber for review/test, red for blocked or destructive.

## Reusable Patterns

- PageHeader: page identity, short operating context, actions, and status pills.
- StatusPill: compact semantic state with optional icon.
- MetricCard: executive count with supporting detail.
- SafetyBanner: visible test/live/blocked/review notices.
- ReadinessPanel: label/value checks before approval, import, or send actions.
- SectionCard: refined content surface for forms, diagnostics, lists, and previews.
